## II. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & TRANSITION FROM LEGAL TO HIGHLY ILLEGAL
This audit traces the complete evidentiary chain of GreenGro Technologies, Inc. (GRNH) common stock transactions from documented private placements in late 2016 through sham consulting agreements, opinion-letter shopping, massive offshore nominee distributions, and subsequent federal felony prosecutions and civil actions.

The evidentiary chronology establishes five distinct operational phases:

### Phase 1: Phase 1: Legal / Nominal Issuance (Oct – Dec 2016)
- **Period:** October 2016 – December 2016
- **Risk Level:** Low / Documented Basis
- **Operational Summary:** Documented direct private placements under Rule 144 / Section 4(a)(1) with valid restricted legends, executed stock purchase agreements (SPA), and documented banking checks from Morgan Stanley ($0.04/share).
- **Key Badges:** Rule 144 Restricted, Documented Consideration, Morgan Stanley Check

### Phase 2: Phase 2: Accelerated Flipping & Nominee Transfers (Early 2017)
- **Period:** January 2017 – May 2017
- **Risk Level:** Elevated / Velocity Anomaly
- **Operational Summary:** Sudden velocity increase. Shares transferred within 4 days of issuance via irrevocable stock powers to Merrill Lynch, Exeter Trust, Cede & Co., and Huntington accounts for open-market liquidation with proceeds wired to personal accounts.
- **Key Badges:** <4 Day Turnaround, Irrevocable Stock Powers, Exeter Trust Wires

### Phase 3: Phase 3: Sham Consulting & Opinion Shopping (Mid 2017 – 2018)
- **Period:** June 2017 – December 2018
- **Risk Level:** High / Regulatory & Securities Law Violation
- **Operational Summary:** Issuance of 25.5M+ shares to shell consultants (Delfin, Rubio, Hulbert, Phoenix Theater) with backdated agreements dating back 4 years to manufacture fake Rule 144 seasoning. Reliance on opinion letters by attorneys Stephen Mills and James Hintzen to strip legends.
- **Key Badges:** 4-Year Backdated Contracts, Section 4(a)(1) Opinion Shopping, 25.5M Advisory Shares

### Phase 4: Phase 4: Massive Offshore Straw Dumping (Early 2019)
- **Period:** January 2019 – May 2019
- **Risk Level:** Critical / Fraudulent Concealment & Section 5 Violations
- **Operational Summary:** Consolidation of over 30,450,000 shares into a single foreign nominee, Paula Marie Poe, at $0.007/share (90% below market), funded via Fidelity Transfer Services and Bank of America accounts, followed by immediate open-market dumping. Haas instructs Sanchez: 'Send 10M to Gaines and 17M back to me.'
- **Key Badges:** 90% Price Discount, Paula Marie Poe Straw, 30.45M Amalgamation

### Phase 5: Phase 5: Federal Indictment & Superior Court Actions (2020 – 2025)
- **Period:** December 2020 – 2025
- **Risk Level:** Criminal Record & Civil Fraud Defense
- **Operational Summary:** Arrest of Ira Gaines on December 15, 2020. 17-count federal indictment in USA v. Gaines (2:21-cr-00266) charging securities fraud, wire fraud, money laundering, and identity theft. Subsequent Maricopa County Superior Court lawsuits (CV2020-015738 and Cheryl Hintzen v. James M. Haas, CV2025-013574) exposing 29M, 78M, and 120M share transfer agent discrepancies.
- **Key Badges:** 17 Federal Felony Counts, Ariz. Super. Ct. CV2025-013574, 120M Discrepancy

## III. PRIMARY STATUTORY FRAUD PATTERNS IDENTIFIED

### Backdated Advisory Contracts (Rule 144 Manufacture)
- **Statutory Violation:** `Securities Act Section 5 / Rule 144(d) Holding Period Fraud`
- **Severity Level:** Critical
- **Analysis:** Agreements executed on May 1, 2017 (e.g. Delfin, Rubio, Hulbert) explicitly backdated the 'Description of Services' starting date by 4 years to September 9, 2013. This created the false documentary appearance of a satisfied 1-year Rule 144 holding period to induce transfer agents to remove restrictive legends.
- **Affected Stock Certificates:** CS1-7910, CS1-7915, CS1-7960-7969, CS1-8002

### VI.E. Statutory Securities Law Violations Framework

- **Summary:** Holding periods can only tack if securities were purchased from non-affiliates. Acquisition of shares or debt from an affiliate (Haas or Gaines) restarts the holding period from day zero.

#### 17 C.F.R. § 230.144(e)(1): Volume Limitations Ceiling
